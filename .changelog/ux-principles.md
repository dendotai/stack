- **UX principles doc** ([docs/ux-principles.md](docs/ux-principles.md), `CLAUDE.md`): the UX patterns every app built from the template follows by default, starting with actions that take an item away (delete, archive, a move to another list): a confirm dialog that names the item, Shift to skip it, focus on the next row, one function per action, and an Undo toast backed by soft delete. A new `## UX` section in `CLAUDE.md` points at the doc before any UX work. To apply: copy `docs/ux-principles.md` and the `## UX` section of `CLAUDE.md`.

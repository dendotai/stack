- **Social providers listed in code** (`packages/api`, `apps/web`, `docs/SETUP.md`, `CONTEXT.md`): `packages/api/convex/socialProviders.ts` lists the social providers a project offers, `["google"]` in the template. `auth.ts` adds the Google provider and requires `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` only while the list names `"google"`, and the login page, which imports the list as `@stack/api/socialProviders`, shows the Google button and its "or" divider only then. A project without Google sign-in sets the list to `[]` and takes `auth.ts` from the template unchanged, with no placeholder Google values on its deployments. To apply: copy `socialProviders.ts`, the `./socialProviders` export in `packages/api/package.json`, `socialProvidersConfig` in `auth.ts`, and the conditional block in `login-page.tsx`; add the "Social provider" entry to `CONTEXT.md`; set the list to the providers your project offers.
